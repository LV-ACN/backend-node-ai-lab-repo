Subject: Can we let properties overbook a bit?

Hey team,

Revenue Management wants to start allowing hotels to accept a few more reservations
than they technically have rooms for, since a chunk of bookings never show up
anyway. We're thinking something like 20% over what's published as available.

A few of our competitors already do this and it's helping their occupancy
numbers. We don't want the booking flow to just let people book forever
though — there needs to be some kind of ceiling, we're just not 100% sure
exactly where that line lives yet (per room type? per property overall?
does it round up or down if the math doesn't come out to a whole room?).

Also heard from the contact center that they sometimes see guests get
confirmed and then we have to call them back later to say we don't actually
have a room — that should never happen with however this gets built.

Can someone on the reservation team figure out the right way to build this
in? We'd like it live before the holiday season booking rush.
